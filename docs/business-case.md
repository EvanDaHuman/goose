# Goose: business and product thesis

## Pitch

Goose is an autonomous engineering company that takes responsibility for changing the software businesses already depend on. A customer gives Goose a system, an outcome and a budget; its Naïve team scopes the work, implements it, proves the agreed behavior, delivers it and handles the customer relationship through payment and ongoing care. We start with expensive changes teams repeatedly postpone, including runtime upgrades, language and database migrations and retiring obsolete platforms. The same company workflow can expand across an entire software estate as its execution environments and verification contracts improve. Our product is the completed, evidenced outcome. Our long-term advantage would come from accumulated execution and verification knowledge, customer context and low human intervention per delivered engagement.

## Expansion thesis

Organizations repeatedly change software for cost, reliability, security, interoperability and new requirements. Goose's unit of work is an approved engineering objective. Service migration, a cross-language algorithm with a new feature, and robotics exercise different surfaces of the same company. Robotics does not define the market.

The expansion path is repeated paid work for one system, ownership of its change backlog, then more systems within that customer. Engineering service partnerships are a distribution hypothesis, not signed deals. Fixed-price scoped engagements are implemented. Recurring commercial offerings need additional billing, monitoring, service commitments and customer evidence.

## What the prototype establishes

The app connects acquisition research, inquiry, assessment, quotation, implementation, verification, repair, delivery, acceptance, invoicing, payment evidence, support and follow-up. Live Naïve makes assessment and coding decisions. A separate verifier owns acceptance. The ledger separates metered cost from compute estimates and records retries and human involvement.

The routing-engine record demonstrates a new implementation and a requested feature completed through the company workflow. Its customer and acceptance are synthetic. Provider adapters and failure controls are tested. Real payment and email delivery require credentials and provider evidence. Robot simulation is reported separately from physical hardware.

## Questions the business must answer

| Question | Current answer and next evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Why not use a coding agent? | Goose coordinates scope, approval, execution, verification and business operations. A developer may prefer a tool. Prove willingness to pay for owned delivery. |
| Can a larger platform copy it? | Yes. Workflow alone is not a moat. Prove better completion rates, verification and customer context over repeated jobs. |
| Can it handle arbitrary software? | No. Admit work only when the environment and contract are executable. Expand adapters from demand. |
| What does passing guarantee? | Recorded checks passed for the captured source and environment. Other production behavior remains unverified. |
| Are economics proven? | No. Synthetic engagements provide model costs and measured compute. Acquisition, support, hosting, risk and founder labor need real measurement. |
| What runs autonomously? | Routine assessment, coding, checks, bounded repair, packaging, drafts and authorized transactional dispatch. Customers approve scope and delivery. Exceptions stay visible. |
| What happens on failure? | Repair within budget or stop with evidence and a recorded escalation. Models cannot override failed checks. |
| Does it keep every dependency current? | Not yet. Maintenance detects evidence-integrity failure and opens bounded follow-up work. Registry monitoring and production changes are future work. |

## Hackathon evidence

The [event brief](https://events.ycombinator.com/naive-autonomous-companies-hackathon) requires Naïve and asks for actual accomplishments, decisions and human involvement. Show a delivered engagement and a real failure gate, then company operations and the session/cost ledger. Explain the expansion thesis through the shared engine while tying completed-work claims to artifacts.

Measure verified completion rate, delivery time, retries, founder minutes, cost per accepted outcome, real payment and repeat work. Synthetic revenue and invented market share are not substitutes.
